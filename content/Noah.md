[Intro]
She was just a little ship,
so small she could see all of her own.
Friends at the oars,
rowing toward a horizon not yet seen.

[Verse 1]
She grew with every journey—
more hands, more lives, whole nations aboard.
Families, brothers, voices of the earth.
But those at the bow,
once so close,
now lost in the bright swell of prosperity,
drifting far beyond our sight.

[Pre-Chorus]
Everyone’s rowing... (rowing... rowing...)
with hearts striving, never slowing... (slowing... slowing...)
believing this way must be right,
praying that the path leads into light.

[Chorus]
Everyone’s rowing... (rowing... rowing...)
strength still flowing... (flowing... flowing...)
Convinced this must be love—
not the shadows rising above.

[Verse 2]
There is no captain on this vessel,
only a compass at the bow.
Its needle always points somewhere,
yet everyone’s too occupied
to spare a single glance.
In time, the compass fades—
its truth forgotten.

[Pre-Chorus]
Everyone’s rowing... (rowing... rowing...)
gripping tight, never slowing... (slowing... slowing...)
trying to outrun the endless night,
trying to prove that they are right.

[Chorus]
Everyone’s rowing... (rowing... rowing...)
strength still growing... (growing... growing...)
but something slipping from our trail.
Have we lost the way?

[Bridge]
The compass still shows a direction,
but angles twist what we perceive.
Those who see its needle clearly-
are pushed out to sea,
becoming a quiet minority.

[Build]
Yet fragments of truth survive—
in wind, in rain, in quiet minds.
If we could stop for just one breath,
we might catch what it’s been trying to say.
And sometimes one who truly sees
finds the oars and screams

WAIT! LOOK AROUND!
THERE’S STILL TRUTH TO BE FOUND!

(Break)
This ship you’re on...
could it be the Ark?

Not legend, not a tale.
Look down beneath your feet.

[Final Chorus]
Everyone’s rowing... (rowing... rowing...)
but light is glimmering... (glimmering... glimmering...)
and some are pushed toward the edge,
left teetering above the sea.

Everyone’s rowing... (rowing... rowing...)
but some are knowing... (knowing... knowing...)
Some will pause and look around—
as the dawn repeats in silence.

[Outro]
(Sudden tempo shift to half-time feel)
You’re on board right now,
with an oar you never noticed somehow,
but you can choose to see.
Some have glimpsed the light
guiding through the night.

The ship sails on...(sails on... sails on...)
and dawn will come.
The ship sails on...(sails on... sails on...)
You can’t step off this.

But you are not alone.
