[Intro]
He says
"If evil can't be wiped away,
I will take the role today."

[Verse 1]
Speeding trap, the second car caught
An urban legend of second-thought
Is evil an alien from the sky?
Or a zombie passing by?

The copycat screams, rough and gruff
"Why am I the one caught in the sea?"
"IF THAT'S FINE, THEN I'M FINE TOO"
Eradicating evil is tough

[Chorus 1]
He asks me
Wanna play like me?
Wanna dance and be free?
Wanna break the decree
And a copycat be?

His makeup is smiling

[Verse 2]
Maybe the guy next door
Or the girl I passed the day before
An innocent child playing in the park
Whose future should be bright, not dark
Imitation is hard to call a sin

[Chorus 2]
He asks me
Wanna lose control?
Wanna play my role?
Is there no one left
With a mimicking soul?

His makeup is crying

[Bridge]
My own madness
Irony
Discontent
Heresy
Ugliness
Resentment

I take them in

[Interlude]

[Build-up]
Colossal ads that fill the screen
Equations erasing what has been
Wars of an unknown, distant age
The silent war of economic rage

Imitation starts to spawn
Infinite division moving on
The future rolls down the slope
"IF THAT'S FINE, THEN I'M FINE TOO"

[Slow down]
He sheds a genuine tear
His makeup breaks and disappears
Only traces remain in sight
As his expression fades
Into the daily light

His task is done
From a quiet height
He looks across the world
And prays

[Drop]
"God, bless their future with grace."

[Interlude]

[Outro]
He says
"Maybe apathy is what gives evil its face."
