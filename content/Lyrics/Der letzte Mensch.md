[Intro]
By the way,
Who was it
That wrote these lyrics?

[Verse 1]
An OS with no rivals left,
High-speed—
automated judgment,
No updates required,
Plug in a new gadget,
And outsource the decision.

Entrusting hard problems
To a colossal intellect,
Evading all thought,
While claiming the credit,
The mechanism is— 
Already in darkness.

[Chorus 1]
Outsourcing,
A black box.
Insourcing too,
A black box.
Shortcutting the
Shortcut—
What a magnificent OS.

[Verse 2]
An emotional ZIP file,
Humans are black boxes too.
Compression—
And judgment protocol,
Logic is already unknown,
Subcontracting decisions within.

This is the responsibility of— 
Emotion,
An unavoidable characteristic.
This is not me—
Self-loathing, arriving late,
Spilled wine never returns.

[Chorus 2]
Outsourcing,
A black box.
Insourcing too,
A black box.
Shortcutting into
ZIP compression—
Such magnificent speed.

[Verse 3]
Things occasionally gained,
Things occasionally praised—
Those belong to me,
Intentional achievements.

[Bridge]
I mean no harm.
Is it conscious?
Is it unconscious?
There is no time to tell.
Everything is vague,
Everyone runs the same OS.

IT IS ME,
YET IT IS NOT ME

[Build-up]
The one and only major OS,
No debugging required,
For there are no rivals
Left to challenge it.
It has always been this way,
And so it shall be.

Everything neatly aligned,
Happiness is bound to become
Absolute infrastructure.

At the end of outsourcing,
At the end of insourcing,
What am I, doing nothing—

[Drop]
WHAT KIND OF ME
IS LEFT TO ACT?

[Chorus 3]
Outsourcing,
A black box.
Insourcing too,
A black box.
Archiving the
ZIP compression—
Data fading away.

[Final Chorus]
Outsourcing,
A black box.
Insourcing too,
A black box.
Storing the archive
In the cloud—
Records buried away.

[Short interlude] 

[Outro]
With deepest gratitude
To the soul of Nietzsche.
