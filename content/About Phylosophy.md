# About Phylosophy

[[SRSBT SYSTEM]]
## Bias
Bias is a network-style narrative.

It is an artificial system for psychological manipulation—a technology capable of being used for either good or evil.
Yet, humans unlock the doors to their minds for this thief.
## Narrative
Stories of the "Accumulation" Type
A portion of the ruminative thoughts that play incessantly in one's mind, like a running narration.

Of course, some stories are necessary; they are essential to human beings.
However, continuing to carry around a "narration that is no longer needed"—even in this day and age—
serves only to limit human potential.
## Humanity
Where is humanity headed, and what is it stumbling over? These are the traces of its trial and error.
In the closed system that is Earth, friction is an inevitable structural consequence.
The aspiration to venture into space emerges as a potential solution.
## Human
Music that invites you to pause and reflect on debugging the "human OS"—a system that hasn’t seen an update since the Stone Age.
While emotions are essential, couldn't the balance of resources allocated to them be restructured?
